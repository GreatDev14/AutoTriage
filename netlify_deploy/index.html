What is Auto Triage?

ADVANCED
VEHICLE AI

Auto Triage is the platform built for every driver. When your car breaks down at 2AM on a busy highway, you open Auto Triage and describe the problem in plain English.

The AI diagnoses it instantly — causes, severity, solutions, cost in USD. Then you call a verified mechanic near you, or book an emergency ride from Uber, Lyft, Bolt, DiDi, Grab or InDrive right from the same screen.

If the AI detects a critical situation — brake failure, engine seizure, fire risk — it immediately surfaces local emergency numbers.
