Verification requirements

READY TO
GET VERIFIED?

AutoTriage verifies every mechanic before drivers see their profile. This protects your reputation, protects drivers, and keeps the network dependable.

01 / Identity

Valid means of verification

We need a reliable identity check so every profile is connected to a real, accountable workshop owner or lead technician.

  • NIN or BVN
  • Another accepted government-issued ID
  • Your submitted details must match your application
02 / Workshop

Workshop and tools verification

Your workspace should be ready to handle the jobs you accept through the network, with the tools and equipment that match your specialty.

  • Certificate of Registration or Incorporation
  • CAC Application Form where registration is pending
  • Workshop and core diagnostic tools may be checked
03 / Contact

An active WhatsApp phone

Repair leads and verification updates move quickly. Your phone must be active and reachable so AutoTriage can contact you when it matters.

  • Working phone number
  • Active WhatsApp line
  • Keep your location and availability current
